How it works

From URL to fully operational AI sales agent in 4 steps
🕷️

1. Crawl Your Store

We scan your WordPress/WooCommerce or Shopify store via public APIs. Products, prices, images, descriptions, categories — everything extracted automatically.

No API keys needed
🧠

2. Train AI Agent

Your product catalog becomes the brain of a conversational sales agent. It understands your inventory, recommends products based on needs, and answers questions naturally.

Conversational AI
💳

3. Connect Payments

Stripe payment links are created for your top products. Customers pay without leaving the chat — credit cards, bank transfers, even cash at OXXO (Mexico).

Stripe Checkout
📣

4. Generate Campaigns

AI researches your market, writes ad copy, creates video scripts, and generates ready-to-post Reels for Instagram and Facebook. Full campaign strategy included.

Meta Ads ready
📱

5. Deploy to WhatsApp

Connect your WhatsApp Business number and your AI agent goes live. Customers chat, browse, buy — all inside WhatsApp. 24/7, no human needed.

WhatsApp Business API
📈

6. Auto-Optimize

Weekly catalog re-crawl, seasonal campaign triggers, A/B ad rotation, and performance-based product recommendations. It gets smarter over time.

Always improving
