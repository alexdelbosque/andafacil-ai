🎬 Demo Scenario — Dummy Walkthrough

Follow these steps to replicate the full experience without completing a real payment

📱 Escenario: María quiere comprar una silla para su papá

  1. Abre el chatbot → WhatsApp Chat
  2. Escribe: "Hola, necesito una silla de ruedas eléctrica para mi papá"
  3. El bot recomienda productos → haz click en "Sillas eléctricas"
  4. El bot muestra la Andafacil Pro con specs y precio ($18,999 MXN)
  5. Escribe: "La quiero comprar"
  6. El bot confirma el pedido y pregunta si generar link de pago
  7. Click en "✅ Sí, generar link"
  8. Aparece un link de pago REAL de Stripe 💳 (no lo completes)
  9. Click en "🎬 Demo: simular pago" para continuar sin pagar
  10. El bot confirma "pago recibido" y pide datos de envío:
  11. → Nombre: "María García López"
  12. → Dirección: "Av. Reforma 123, Col. Centro"
  13. → Ciudad: "Ciudad de México, CDMX"
  14. → CP: "06000"
  15. → Teléfono: "55 1234 5678"
  16. → Notas: "Edificio B, depto 4A"
  17. 🎉 El bot muestra el resumen completo del pedido

📦 What the Agent Does

1

🕷️ Crawls Your WordPress Store

Automatically scrapes all 66 products from andafacil.com via the WordPress REST API. Extracts names, descriptions, specs, prices, images, categories, and stock levels. No API keys needed.

2

💬 WhatsApp Sales Chatbot

Answers any product question, recommends products based on needs ("my mom needs help walking"), and uses real customer testimonials. Speaks Mexican Spanish naturally.

3

💳 Stripe Payment Links

Generates real Stripe payment links (MXN) for any product. Accepts credit cards, OXXO, and SPEI. After payment, collects delivery address directly in chat.

4

🔬 Audience Research

1,118-line strategy brief analyzing the Mexican seniors/mobility market. Emotional hooks, targeting strategies, seasonal calendar, competitor analysis. Finding: nobody owns D2C mobility on social in Mexico.

5

🎬 Ad Campaign Generator

18 Meta ad campaigns (6 products × 3 angles) with complete video scripts (second-by-second), ad copy in Spanish, targeting recommendations, and carousel layouts.

6

⏰ Automated Refresh

Cron jobs for weekly ad refresh, seasonal campaign triggers (Día de las Madres, Buen Fin), and re-crawling the catalog for new products.
