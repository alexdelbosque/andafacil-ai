## 1. Market Context & Digital Landscape

### Mexico Digital Stats (2024 DataReportal)
- **128.9 million** total population
- **107.3 million** internet users (83.2% penetration)
- **90.2 million** social media users (70% of population)
- **90.2 million** Facebook users (the dominant platform)
- **83.1 million** YouTube users
- **51.7% female** / 48.3% male on Facebook
- **98.1%** of adults 18+ use at least one social media platform
- Mobile-first market: median mobile speed 25 Mbps

### Key Demographics for Andafacil
- **8.7% of population is 65+** (~11.2 million people) — growing fast
- **14.1% aged 35–44** + **12.1% aged 45–54** = **~33.8 million** adults in the buyer demographic
- Facebook is the #1 platform for 45+ in Mexico (massive adoption, unlike US where younger users have migrated away)
- WhatsApp is the dominant messaging platform (key for closing sales)
- Instagram skews younger but is growing rapidly in the 35–55 segment

### Why This Market Matters
- Mexico's aging population is growing faster than its healthcare infrastructure
- Limited public mobility aid programs = massive private market opportunity
- Cultural norm: families care for elderly parents at home (not nursing homes)
- Adult children are the primary purchasers and decision-makers
- The mobility equipment market in Mexico is fragmented — no dominant D2C brand with strong social presence
- Lower CPMs than US/Europe = more affordable customer acquisition

## 2. Best Performing Meta Ad Formats

### Format Rankings by Audience Segment

#### For Adult Children (35–55) — THE PRIMARY BUYER
| Rank | Format | Why It Works | Best For |
|------|--------|-------------|----------|
| 🥇 | **Video (Feed)** | Highest emotional impact, allows storytelling | Awareness, consideration |
| 🥈 | **Carousel** | Show product features + before/after + testimonials in one ad | Conversion, retargeting |
| 🥉 | **Reels** | High organic reach, algorithm boost, catches scroll | Awareness, reach |
| 4 | **Stories** | Full-screen immersive, good for limited offers | Retargeting, urgency |
| 5 | **Single Image (Feed)** | Clean product showcase with strong copy | Retargeting, catalog |

#### For Seniors (65+) — THE END USER
| Rank | Format | Why It Works | Best For |
|------|--------|-------------|----------|
| 🥇 | **Single Image (Feed)** | Simple, clear, easy to read — no swiping required | All stages |
| 🥈 | **Video (Feed)** | Demonstrations, testimonials from peers | Consideration |
| 🥉 | **Carousel** | Step-by-step product features | Education |

### Format-Specific Recommendations

**Video Feed Ads (Primary format — 60% of budget)**
- Best for emotional storytelling that drives the adult-child buyer
- Ideal length: 15–30 seconds (see Section 7 for details)
- Always add captions/subtitles (many watch without sound)
- Use 4:5 aspect ratio for maximum feed real estate

**Carousel Ads (20% of budget)**
- Card 1: Emotional hook image (grandparent with family)
- Card 2: Product hero shot
- Cards 3–4: Key features/benefits
- Card 5: Social proof (star rating, review quote)
- Final card: CTA with pricing or offer
- USE: Product comparisons, "before/after" lifestyle, feature breakdowns

**Reels (15% of budget)**
- 9:16 vertical format
- Hook in first 1.5 seconds
- Best for: unboxing, "watch abuelito's reaction," day-in-the-life
- Natural, authentic feel (not overproduced)
- Trending audio when culturally appropriate

**Stories (5% of budget)**
- Use primarily for retargeting warm audiences
- Flash sales, countdown timers for seasonal events
- Swipe-up CTA to WhatsApp or product page

### Critical Technical Notes
- **Always use Advantage+ placements** but review performance by placement weekly
- **Automatic placements** let Meta optimize, but ensure creative works in all formats
- Create **platform-native versions** — don't just resize one creative
- Facebook Feed remains the #1 revenue driver for 35+ in Mexico

## 3. Emotional Hooks That Drive Conversions

### The Dual-Audience Emotional Framework

Mobility products are almost never impulse purchases. They're emotional, research-heavy, and often purchased during a **crisis moment** (a fall, a diagnosis, a decline in mobility). Understanding the emotional triggers for each audience is essential.

### For Adult Children (35–55) — "The Gift-Giver / Caretaker"

| Hook | Emotional Trigger | Example Angle | Effectiveness |
|------|------------------|---------------|--------------|
| 🎁 **Gift of Freedom** | Love, generosity, making parents happy | "Dale a tu mamá el regalo de volver a caminar con libertad" | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| 🛡️ **Safety / Peace of Mind** | Fear, anxiety about falls/injury | "Cada 11 segundos un adulto mayor sufre una caída. Protege a quien te protegió" | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 **Family Unity** | Togetherness, inclusion | "Que vuelva a sentarse a la mesa con toda la familia" | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| 💪 **Independence for Parent** | Relief from guilt, reducing burden | "No necesita depender de nadie para moverse en su propia casa" | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| 🙏 **Duty / Reciprocity** | Cultural obligation, gratitude | "Ellos dieron todo por ti. Ahora te toca a ti." | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| ⏰ **Urgency / Regret Avoidance** | Fear of not acting in time | "No esperes a la siguiente caída" | ⭐⭐⭐ |

### For Seniors (65+) — "The User"

| Hook | Emotional Trigger | Example Angle | Effectiveness |
|------|------------------|---------------|--------------|
| 🦅 **Independence / Dignity** | Pride, self-sufficiency | "Vuelve a hacer lo que amas, sin pedir ayuda" | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| 😊 **Joy / Lifestyle** | Happiness, social connection | "Vuelve a disfrutar del parque con tus nietos" | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| 🚶 **Mobility = Life** | Freedom, movement | "La vida no se detiene. Tú tampoco." | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| 🏠 **Stay in Your Home** | Comfort, resistance to change | "Sigue viviendo en tu hogar, con total seguridad" | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |
| 💊 **Pain Relief / Comfort** | Physical comfort | "Camina sin dolor, muévete con confianza" | ⭐⭐⭐ |

### Top 5 Winning Hook Formulas (Tested Patterns)

1. **"Regalo de independencia"** — Position the product as the ultimate gift
   - *"El mejor regalo para mamá no cabe en una caja"*

2. **"El miedo a la caída"** — Fear-based but solution-focused
   - *"1 de cada 3 adultos mayores sufre una caída cada año. La prevención empieza hoy."*

3. **"La reacción"** — Show the emotional moment of receiving the product
   - *Video: Adult child surprises parent with wheelchair/walker → tears of joy*

4. **"Antes y después"** — Transformation story
   - *"Hace 3 meses, Don José no podía salir de su cuarto. Hoy pasea por el parque."*

5. **"No más depender"** — Independence narrative
   - *"Dejó de pedir ayuda para ir al baño. Ahora se mueve solo."*

### ⚠️ Hooks to AVOID
- **Pity / victimization** — Mexican culture respects elders; never make them look helpless
- **Clinical / hospital imagery** — Products should feel lifestyle, not medical
- **Fear without solution** — Always end with hope and empowerment
- **Age-shaming language** — "viejo/a" is sensitive; use "adulto mayor" or names/roles (abuelito/a, mamá, papá)

## 4. UGC vs Polished Content

### The Verdict: **UGC Wins for Conversion, Polished Wins for Brand**

In the mobility equipment space, the data is clear across markets:

| Metric | UGC | Polished/Brand |
|--------|-----|----------------|
| Click-Through Rate (CTR) | **73% higher** | Baseline |
| Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) | **29% lower** | Baseline |
| Trust/Credibility | **⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐** | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| Brand Perception | ⭐⭐⭐ | **⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐** |
| Engagement Rate | **2–3x higher** | Baseline |
| Shareability | **Much higher** (family shares) | Lower |

### Recommended Creative Mix

| Content Type | % of Creative Output | Use Case |
|-------------|---------------------|----------|
| **Customer UGC (real testimonials)** | 40% | Conversion campaigns, social proof |
| **Semi-polished UGC-style** (brand-produced but looks authentic) | 30% | Awareness + consideration |
| **Polished brand content** | 20% | Brand awareness, website hero, catalog |
| **Employee/founder content** | 10% | Trust-building, behind-the-scenes |

### UGC Content Ideas for Andafacil

1. **"Mi abuelito probó la silla por primera vez"** — Unboxing/first-use reaction video
2. **Customer WhatsApp video testimonials** — Ask buyers to send 30-second clips
3. **"Un día con Don [Nombre]"** — Day-in-the-life mini-documentary (1 min)
4. **Family moments** — Grandparent playing with grandkids thanks to mobility aid
5. **Before/after montage** — Side-by-side of struggle vs. ease with product
6. **"Mi mamá me lo pidió"** — Adult child telling story of why they bought it
7. **Product demo by real user** — Senior showing how easy it is to fold/unfold/use

### How to Collect UGC
- **Post-purchase WhatsApp message**: "¡Nos encantaría ver cómo le va a [nombre]! Mándanos un videíto y te damos 10% de descuento en tu próxima compra" 🎥
- **QR code in packaging**: Links to simple video upload form
- **Facebook group**: Create "Comunidad Andafacil" for users to share stories
- **Incentivized reviews**: Offer small discount for video reviews
- **Delivery team captures**: Train delivery personnel to ask for permission to film setup moment

### Semi-Polished UGC Production Tips
- Shoot on iPhone, not cinema cameras
- Natural lighting (living rooms, parks, kitchens)
- Real people (not models) — hire actual seniors and their families
- Imperfect framing feels more authentic
- Add captions/subtitles but keep the raw audio
- No stock music — real ambient sound or trending audio for Reels

## 6. Targeting Strategies

**Audience 1: Adult Children — Direct Interest**
- Age: 35–55
- Location: Mexico (urban metros: CDMX, Guadalajara, Monterrey, Puebla, Querétaro)

  - "Home healthcare" OR "Elder care"

**Audience 2: Adult Children — Behavioral/Contextual**
- Age: 35–55
- Location: Mexico
- Interests:
  - "Mother's Day gifts" OR "Father's Day gifts"
  - "Caregiver" OR "Cuidado del adulto mayor"
  - "IMSS" OR "ISSSTE" (people researching public health = likely have aging parents)
  - "Geriatría" OR "Gerontología"
- Behaviors:
  - Online shoppers
  - Engaged shoppers (Meta pixel data)

**Audience 3: Adult Children — Life Stage**
- Age: 35–55
- Location: Mexico
- Interests:
  - Parents with adult children (18+)
  - Interested in "elderly care" OR "senior living"
  - Engaged with health & wellness content
  - Combined with income indicators (iPhone users, specific neighborhoods)

**Audience 4: Seniors — Direct**
- Age: 60–75 (start at 60 to catch early adopters)
- Location: Mexico
- Interests:
  - Health & wellness
  - Walking / exercise
  - Retirement
  - "Salud" OR "Bienestar"

**Audience 5: Healthcare Professionals (Referral)**
- Age: 25–55
- Location: Mexico
- Interests:
  - Physical therapy / Fisioterapia
  - Orthopedics / Ortopedia
  - Geriatric medicine

### Targeting Best Practices

1. **Start broad, then narrow** — Let Meta's algorithm learn before restricting too much
2. **Use Advantage+ Audience** for conversion campaigns (Meta's AI finds buyers efficiently)
3. **Layer interests** — Don't OR everything together; create separate ad sets to test which interest clusters perform
4. **Geographic focus**: Start with CDMX, Monterrey, Guadalajara, Puebla, Querétaro, Mérida (highest purchasing power + logistics reach)
5. **Exclude existing customers** from acquisition campaigns
6. **Age-stacked ad sets**: Create separate ad sets for 35–44, 45–55, and 60+ to tailor messaging
7. **Dayparting**: Mexican seniors are most active 7–11 AM and 6–9 PM on Facebook; adult children 12–2 PM and 8–11 PM
8. **Device targeting**: For seniors, bias toward Android (80%+ of Mexican mobile users) and desktop

### Advanced: WhatsApp Integration
- Use **Click-to-WhatsApp ads** as primary conversion objective
- In Mexico, many high-consideration purchases close via WhatsApp conversation
- Set up automated greeting: "Hola 👋 ¿En qué podemos ayudarte? Cuéntanos sobre tu familiar y te recomendamos el mejor producto"
- Track WhatsApp conversions as primary KPI

## 7. Video Ad Best Practices

### The 3-Second Rule

> **You have 3 seconds to stop the scroll.** If you don't hook them in 3 seconds, you've lost them.

### Hook Strategies (First 3 Seconds)

| Hook Type | Example | When to Use |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| **Pattern Interrupt** | Close-up of elderly hands gripping a table for balance | Awareness campaigns |
| **Emotional Reaction** | Grandparent seeing wheelchair for first time, tears of joy | Gift-giving campaigns |
| **Bold Text Overlay** | "Tu mamá NO debería tener miedo de caminar" | Fear → solution |
| **Question** | "¿Sabías que las caídas son la #1 causa de lesión en adultos mayores?" | Educational |
| **Before/After** | Split screen: struggling to walk → gliding smoothly | Transformation |
| **Unboxing Start** | Hands opening a box → product reveal | Product consideration |
| **Social Proof** | "2,500+ familias mexicanas confían en nosotros" | Trust-building |

### Optimal Video Lengths

| Funnel Stage | Optimal Length | Purpose |
|-------------|---------------|---------|
| **Top of Funnel (Awareness)** | 6–15 seconds | Stop scroll, introduce brand |
| **Middle of Funnel (Consideration)** | 15–30 seconds | Tell story, show product benefits |
| **Bottom of Funnel (Conversion)** | 30–60 seconds | Testimonials, detailed demo, overcome objections |
| **Reels** | 15–30 seconds | Awareness, organic reach boost |

### Video Structure Templates

#### Template A: "The Gift Reveal" (30 sec)
```
0–3s:  HOOK — "Le dije a mi mamá que le tenía algo..." (close-up of wrapping)
3–8s:  BUILD — Walking toward mom, handing the product
8–15s: PAYOFF — Mom's emotional reaction, trying the product
15–25s: PRODUCT — Quick feature callouts (text overlays)
25–30s: CTA — "Regálale libertad. Link en bio / Escríbenos por WhatsApp"
```

#### Template B: "The Problem → Solution" (20 sec)
```
0–3s:  HOOK — Elderly person struggling (dramatic, but dignified)
3–8s:  PROBLEM — Text: "1 de cada 3 adultos mayores sufre una caída"
8–15s: SOLUTION — Product in action, person moving freely
15–20s: CTA — Price, free shipping, WhatsApp link
```

#### Template C: "Day in the Life" (45 sec)
```
0–3s:  HOOK — "Antes, mi papá no salía de su cuarto..."
3–15s: BEFORE — Gentle portrayal of limitation (with dignity)
15–30s: TRANSFORMATION — With Andafacil product, doing daily activities
30–40s: TESTIMONIAL — Real words from family member
40–45s: CTA — Brand, link, WhatsApp
```

#### Template D: "Product Demo" (20 sec)
```
0–3s:  HOOK — Product fold/unfold in 1 second (satisfying visual)
3–10s: FEATURES — Weight, fold, safety features (text overlays)
10–15s: IN USE — Real person using it smoothly
15–20s: CTA — Price + "Envío gratis a todo México"
```

### Technical Specifications

- **Aspect Ratio**: 4:5 for Feed (primary), 9:16 for Reels/Stories, 1:1 for cross-placement
- **Resolution**: Minimum 1080x1350 (4:5), ideally shoot in 4K and resize
- **Captions**: ALWAYS — 85% of Facebook videos are watched without sound. Use large, readable text (not auto-generated; manually edit)
- **Caption Style**: White text with black background bar (highest readability for seniors)
- **Branding**: Logo watermark in corner from second 3 onward (not first 3 — don't waste hook time)
- **Thumbnail**: Custom thumbnail for Feed placement (not auto-selected frame)
- **File Size**: Keep under 100MB; compress appropriately
- **CTA Placement**: On-screen text CTA at 75% mark AND end; verbal CTA at end

### Video Production Priority List (Start Here)
1. **3 customer testimonial videos** (highest converting)
2. **1 "gift reaction" video** (highest share rate)
3. **2 product demo videos** (for consideration stage)
4. **1 "day in the life" mini-doc** (brand awareness)
5. **5 short Reels** (15 sec, UGC-style, trend-based)

## 10. Testimonial-Based Ad Strategies

### Why Testimonials Are Gold in This Category

- **92% of consumers** trust recommendations from others over branded content
- Healthcare/mobility purchases are **high-trust, high-anxiety** decisions
- Mexican culture is heavily **word-of-mouth driven** — "mi comadre me recomendó" is the strongest signal
- **Family buyers need reassurance** that they're making the right choice for their parent

### Types of Testimonials to Collect

| Type | Format | Conversion Power | Difficulty |
|------|--------|-----------------|------------|
| **Video testimonial from family** | 30–60 sec video | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Medium |
| **Video testimonial from user** | 30–60 sec video | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Medium-High |
| **Photo + written review** | Image ad with quote | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Easy |
| **WhatsApp screenshot** | Image with real chat | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Easy |
| **Before/after photo set** | Carousel ad | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Medium |
| **Star rating aggregation** | Text in ad copy | ⭐⭐⭐ | Easy |
| **Doctor/therapist endorsement** | Video or quote card | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Hard |

### Testimonial Video Framework

**The Perfect 45-Second Testimonial:**
```
0–5s:   NAME + CONTEXT — "Soy María, de Guadalajara. Mi papá tiene 78 años."
5–15s:  THE PROBLEM — "Hace un año, tuvo una caída y dejó de caminar."
15–25s: THE DISCOVERY — "Una amiga me recomendó Andafacil..."
25–35s: THE RESULT — "Ahora sale al parque todos los días. Ya hasta camina al tianguis."
35–40s: THE EMOTION — "Ver a mi papá sonreír otra vez... no tiene precio."
40–45s: THE RECOMMENDATION — "Si tienes un familiar que lo necesita, no lo pienses."
```

### How to Leverage Testimonials in Ads

#### Strategy 1: Testimonial Compilation Reel
- Compile 5–8 short clips (3–5 sec each) of different customers
- Text overlay with name and city
- End with: "Más de X familias confían en nosotros. ¿La tuya será la siguiente?"
- Works great as a Reel or Feed video

#### Strategy 2: Quote Card Carousel
- Each carousel card = one review with customer photo
- Card 1: "Lo que dicen nuestras familias..."
- Cards 2–5: Individual reviews with star ratings
- Final card: CTA + product lineup

#### Strategy 3: WhatsApp Screenshot Ad
- Screenshot of real WhatsApp conversation (with permission)
- Shows genuine excitement/gratitude
- Blur personal info but keep the raw, authentic feel
- Caption: "Esto nos motiva cada día 🥺 ¿Quieres que tu familiar se sienta así?"

#### Strategy 4: "Before & After" Testimonial Video
- 15-sec video split: left side = before (struggling), right side = after (with product)
- Voice-over from family member
- Extremely shareable format

#### Strategy 5: Aggregated Social Proof in Every Ad
- Include in ALL ad copy: "⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4.9/5 — X reseñas verificadas"
- Shows legitimacy without needing full testimonial

### Testimonial Collection System

1. **Day of Delivery**: Delivery team asks for photo with product (offer small gift)
2. **3 Days After**: WhatsApp message — "¿Cómo le ha ido a [nombre] con su [producto]?"
3. **2 Weeks After**: Request for video testimonial (offer 10% next purchase discount)
4. **Monthly**: "Historia del Mes" — feature one family's story in-depth
5. **Ongoing**: Facebook group where customers share organically

### Legal Considerations
- ALWAYS get written permission (WhatsApp consent is sufficient in Mexico)
- Simple message: "¿Nos permites compartir tu experiencia en nuestras redes para ayudar a otras familias?"
- For video: brief verbal consent on camera is ideal
- Comply with LFPDPPP (Mexico's data protection law) — get explicit consent for use in advertising

## 11. Spanish-Language Ad Copy Tips

### Tú vs. Usted: The Critical Decision

| Audience | Pronoun | Tone | Rationale |
|----------|---------|------|-----------|
| Adult Children (35–55) | **Tú** | Warm, familiar, conversational | They're your peers; "tú" feels like a friend's advice |
| Seniors (65+) | **Usted** | Respectful, formal, dignified | Mexican culture demands respect for elders; "usted" is expected |
| Mixed/General | **Tú** (addressing the buyer) | Familiar with respectful undertone | "Dale a tu mamá..." — "tú" for the buyer about their parent |

### Key Rule: When talking TO seniors, use usted. When talking to their children ABOUT seniors, use tú.

**Example:**
- To adult child: "¿**Tu** mamá necesita ayuda para caminar? **Dale** la libertad que **se merece**."
- To senior: "**Usted** merece moverse con libertad. **Conozca** nuestros productos."

### Emotional Vocabulary That Converts in Mexican Spanish

| English Concept | ❌ Avoid | ✅ Use Instead | Why |
|----------------|----------|---------------|-----|
| Elderly person | Viejo/a, Anciano/a | **Adulto mayor**, Abuelito/a, Persona mayor | "Viejo" can feel dismissive; "abuelito/a" is affectionate |
| Wheelchair | (Just use it) | **Silla de ruedas**, Silla motorizada | Standard terms |
| Walker | (none) | **Andador**, Andadera | Both common; "andador" preferred |
| Independence | Independencia | **Libertad**, Independencia, Autonomía | "Libertad" has deeper emotional resonance |
| Safe | Seguro | **Seguro/a**, Protegido/a, Con confianza | "Con confianza" implies empowerment |
| Help | Ayuda | **Apoyo**, Respaldo | "Apoyo" feels less patronizing than "ayuda" |
| Disability | Discapacidad | **Movilidad reducida**, Dificultad para caminar | More dignified framing |
| Old age | Vejez | **Edad dorada**, Plenitud, Esta etapa de la vida | Positive framing |
| Fall | Caída | **Caída**, Accidente, Tropiezo | Direct is fine for fear-based hooks |

### Mexican-Specific Cultural Triggers

1. **"Familia primero"** — Family is everything. Position the product as a family investment, not personal purchase.

2. **"No ser una carga"** — Seniors deeply fear being a burden. Frame the product as giving them independence so they're NOT a burden. This is the most powerful trigger for senior-targeted ads.

3. **"Respeto a los mayores"** — Deep cultural respect for elders. Never portray them as helpless, weak, or pitiful. Show them as dignified, capable, valued.

4. **"Compadrazgo / Word of mouth"** — Recommendations from trusted people (family, friends, comadres) carry immense weight. This is why testimonial ads and community building are essential.

5. **"Desconfianza online"** — Many Mexican consumers (especially 50+) are wary of buying online. Overcome with: money-back guarantee, WhatsApp personal attention, real-person customer service, verified reviews, physical address shown.

6. **"Meses sin intereses" (MSI)** — Interest-free monthly payments are EXPECTED for purchases above ~$2,000 MXN. Not offering MSI is a conversion killer. Partner with Mercado Pago, Kueski Pay, or bank installment plans.

7. **"El doctor dijo..."** — Doctor/authority recommendations carry significant weight. If you can get healthcare professional endorsements, lead with them.

### Copy Tone Guide

**DO:**
- Write like you're talking to a friend who needs advice
- Use conversational, warm Mexican Spanish (not Spain-Spanish)
- Include colloquialisms appropriate to the context: "échale un vistazo," "no lo pienses más"
- Use diminutives warmly: "abuelito," "pasito a pasito" (but don't overdo it)
- Be direct about prices, shipping, and guarantees
- End with a clear, single CTA

**DON'T:**
- Use Spain-Spanish constructions ("coged," "vosotros")
- Be overly clinical or medical in tone
- Use corporate jargon ("soluciones integrales de movilidad")
- Patronize or infantilize seniors
- Use complex sentence structures — keep it simple and scannable
- Forget that Mexican humor is warm and self-deprecating — occasional lightness works

### Headline Formulas in Spanish

1. **Pregunta directa**: "¿Tu mamá tiene miedo de caerse?" 
2. **Estadística impactante**: "1 de cada 3 adultos mayores sufre una caída cada año"
3. **Testimonio corto**: "'Mi papá volvió a salir al parque' — María G."
4. **Beneficio claro**: "Ligero, seguro y se dobla en 1 segundo"
5. **Urgencia**: "Solo esta semana: envío gratis + 20% de descuento"
6. **Emocional**: "El mejor regalo no cabe en una caja 🎁"
7. **Curiosidad**: "Esto es lo que pasa cuando le das libertad a tu familiar"
